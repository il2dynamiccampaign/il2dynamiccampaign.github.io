Before you start

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • IL-2 Great Battles with the Stalingrad map.
  • About 1 GB of free disk space.

Installation

  1. Download the ZIP and extract it anywhere.
  2. Double click IL-2 Dynamic Campaign.exe.
  3. Follow the setup guide to choose your IL-2 folder.
